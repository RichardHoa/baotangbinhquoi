# Bình Quới Eco-Museum

An interactive map of the Thanh Đa - Bình Quới peninsula that presents its wildlife survey records and its people.

## Language

**Sighting**:
One survey record of a species at a specific place, date and time. A sighting may have no photo of its own.
_Avoid_: Record, observation, animal

**Pin**:
The map marker for one sighting or one con người entry. A species seen several times has several pins.
_Avoid_: Marker, point

**Động vật**:
The legend category covering every sighting: birds, reptiles and amphibians. Its pins take their colour from their species and their shape from their group.
_Avoid_: Animal, wildlife

**Con người**:
The legend category for people on the peninsula. Its pins are yellow; what one entry represents is not yet defined.
_Avoid_: Human, people

**Species**:
One kind of animal recorded in the survey, such as Collared Kingfisher or _Kaloula pulchra_. Each species belongs to one group (Chim, Bò sát or Lưỡng cư) and has its own colour on the map.
_Avoid_: Animal, loài entry

**Species profile**:
The view of one species: its photos, names, group and a short text on its appearance, behaviour and habitat. Opened from the legend or from any of the species' pins.
_Avoid_: Species card, species popup, detail

**Species photo**:
A photo whose filename matches a survey row. A species needs at least one to appear on the map. A filename with a sighting ID belongs to that sighting; a filename with a species name, as all bird photos have, belongs to every sighting of that species. The species profile shows all of a species' photos, each once.
_Avoid_: Sighting photo, image

**Unplaced species**:
A species that has a species photo but no sighting, so it has no location and does not appear on the map.
_Avoid_: Missing species, unmatched bird
